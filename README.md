## About Me

Computer engineer and full-stack developer building products that solve real problems. I design, build, and ship web applications from zero to production — handling everything from database architecture to pixel-perfect interfaces.

I focus on developer experience, performance, and shipping fast. My work spans SaaS platforms, browser extensions, community tools, and open-source contributions.

## Projects

### [Prolific Tea](https://prolifictea.com)

A community platform where Prolific research participants review researchers, track study outcomes, and share real hourly pay data. Includes a Chrome extension that surfaces researcher ratings directly on Prolific's study page.

### [AlertaCert](https://alertacert.com)

Certificate management infrastructure for accounting firms in Latin America. Manages digital certificates (e.firma, DIAN, AFIP) with encrypted storage, expiry alerts, real-time validation, and PDF signing.

### [Nextron](https://github.com/saltyshiomix/nextron) (Open Source Contributor)

Shipped Next.js 16 + React 19 support to Nextron, a framework for building Electron desktop apps with Next.js. The contribution touched 33 files, enabled full Turbopack compatibility, and released as `nextron@9.6.0` the same day it merged.

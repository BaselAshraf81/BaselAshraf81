## Eigendrum

**[eigendrum.com](https://eigendrum.com)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/BaselAshraf81/eigendrum)

Draw any shape and hear the sound it would actually make. Eigendrum treats your outline as a drumhead clamped at the rim, solves the Dirichlet eigenvalue problem for the Laplacian on that exact region with P1 finite elements, and synthesises the frequencies it finds. No samples, no hand-tuned oscillators. Every frequency ratio you hear was computed from the shape you drew.

- Draw a circle and the overtones land on **Bessel function zeros**. Draw a square and they land on `sqrt(m² + n²)`.
- Strike it anywhere: the timbre changes with position, because a mode with a nodal line under the mallet cannot be excited at all. That falls out of the physics rather than being coded in.
- Both halves of the **Gordon-Webb-Wolpert isospectral pair** are built in, so you can hear two different shapes that sound identical. Measured agreement on the lowest 16 modes: within `1.07e-7`%.
- The solver is checked against closed-form spectra on every commit. Worst error over the lowest 8 modes at 6000 nodes: **0.16%**, with clean second-order convergence.

Zero runtime dependencies, no build step, no backend. Reached **#1 on r/InternetIsBeautiful** and the front page of Hacker News.
